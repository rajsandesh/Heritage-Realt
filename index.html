Our Story

Simplifying Modernist Living, One Landmark at a Time.

We built Heritage Realty to make radical architectural homes possible for those who refuse generic luxury. From steep Sedona red rocks to coastal Malibu horizons, our goal is to eliminate structural friction and turn unyielding topography into serene living space.

By blending drone LiDAR surveying with artisanal board-formed concrete and custom unlacquered metallurgy, our atelier creates spaces that speak for themselves before you even open the door.

FAQ

I've worked with developers before, and they compromised on materials. How are you different?

+
Every residence commissioned through Heritage Realty operates with guaranteed material purity. We source our raw limestone directly from Mediterranean quarries and execute our board-formed concrete with custom architectural timber molds. No synthetic composites are ever utilized.

How do I know this architectural acquisition will hold its value?

+
Architecturally significant properties represent finite historical assets. Our residences consistently outpace standardized luxury developments in appreciation because they are engineered to age organically with natural patina rather than requiring cyclical cosmetic renovation.

Do you handle discreet, off-market property representations?

+
Yes. Over 65% of our portfolio transactions are handled off-market under strict non-disclosure agreements to preserve the total privacy of our buyers and sellers.

Can your studio manage raw land acquisition and site feasibility?

+
Our specialized development advisory manages every element of early land scouting: drone LiDAR topographical surveys, bedrock core sampling, solar trajectory modeling, and local municipal zoning approvals.

Do you oversee both design conception and construction execution?

+
Heritage Realty is a full-spectrum atelier. We provide complete structural engineering blueprints, volumetric 3D analysis, and direct on-site general construction oversight to ensure millimeter precision.

Will I need to micromanage the engineering and permitting process?

+
No. Our patrons receive transparent milestone reporting and digital twin visual updates while our project directors manage all structural calculations, permits, and subcontractor logistics.

How do we begin a private commission with Heritage Realty?

+
You can initiate a confidential dialogue via our contact portal or by direct consultation request with our concierge desk in Zurich, Los Angeles, or Dubai.
